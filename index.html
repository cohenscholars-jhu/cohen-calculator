Understanding the service obligation rules

Why are summers excluded?

Per clarification from the Maryland Higher Education Commission and the Nurse Support Program II, only Fall and Spring semesters count toward incurring or fulfilling service time. Summer Cohen funding does not add service time and cannot be used to fulfill it. Summer funding may, however, factor into the dollar amount subject to repayment if service requirements are not met. That is a separate calculation handled by the program office.

How service time is calculated

Each funded Fall or Spring semester equals 0.5 year of service obligation:

  • One semester of full-time service, or
  • Two semesters of part-time service.

For example, four funded Fall/Spring semesters would equal 2 years of full-time service or 4 years of part-time service.

Where can service be completed?

At an approved Maryland institution granting nursing degrees, or an approved Maryland hospital educator or professional development role. Only Maryland sites qualify. Out-of-state institutions do not count.

When must service begin?

Within 6 months after completion of your degree or certificate program. Service cannot be fulfilled before program completion. Post-graduation service must be continuous across Fall and Spring at a single site.

What about leaves of absence or self-pay semesters?

A semester without Cohen funding does not create service obligation. If you have a self-pay semester, a leave of absence, or a gap in funding, leave that semester unchecked in Step 2. Only semesters with confirmed Cohen funding count.

What if my situation is unusual?

This calculator works best for scholars with straightforward funding records and service plans. If your situation includes mid-program gaps, overlapping grants, or anything that does not fit the usual pattern, please contact the program office for an individualized review.

What is the official source of truth?

Your signed Service Obligation Agreement, Promissory Note, and the Institutional Student Funding Record (ISFR) prepared after graduation are the authoritative documents. This calculator is a planning aid and is not a replacement for those records.

Questions about your service obligation?
If your results look off, your situation is complex, or you just want confirmation, please reach out. We are happy to help.
